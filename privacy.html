Mobile Application Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 4, 2026  |  Application: Freestone County ARES (iOS & Android)

1. Introduction & Mission

Freestone County Amateur Radio Emergency Service ("Freestone County ARES", "we", "us", or "our") is a non-profit volunteer public service organization providing auxiliary emergency telecommunications support to local government, emergency management coordinators, first responders, and served non-governmental relief agencies during severe weather emergencies, natural disasters, and civic events.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application ("Freestone County ARES") handles personal and operational data. We hold our volunteers' privacy in high regard: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade any personal or location data to commercial advertisers or data brokers.

2. Information We Collect

To facilitate emergency communications and directed net check-ins, the application may collect:

A. Volunteer Profile & Credentials

  • FCC Amateur Radio Call Sign, License Class (Technician, General, Amateur Extra), and GMRS Call Sign.
  • Contact Information: Full Name, telephone number (for tactical paging/SMS mobilization), and email address (fcares@pm.me / volunteer email).
  • FEMA / NIMS Certifications: IS-100, IS-200, IS-700, IS-800 completion records and Skywarn Spotter identification.
  • Equipment Capabilities: Radio capabilities (VHF/UHF mobile, Handheld Transceiver, HF portable, Winlink RMS packet station, portable generator/solar capacity).

B. Location Data (During Active Operations Only)

When enabled by the user during an active incident, weekly training net, or Skywarn severe weather observation, the app accesses precise device coordinates to compute your Maidenhead Grid Square (e.g., EM11xq) and plot your operational station on tactical incident maps.

No Background Tracking: Location is never gathered passively in the background when the app is inactive or closed, unless the operator explicitly engages active incident tracking during an emergency deployment.

C. Emergency Notification & Device Diagnostics

  • Push notification device tokens (Apple Push Notification Service / Firebase) to deliver urgent incident paging tones.
  • Device OS version and error crash reports to maintain application reliability during mission-critical field operations.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected is used strictly for public safety communications, including:

  • Emergency Paging: Alerting operators to immediate weather standbys or county EOC activations.
  • Directed Net Operations: Conducting formal check-ins for the weekly Sunday ARES net (2000 Hours) and emergency traffic nets.
  • ICS Form Generation: Automating FEMA Incident Command System forms (ICS-205 Communications Annex, ICS-214 Activity Log).
  • Skywarn Reporting: Coordinating ground-truth storm reports with the National Weather Service.

4. Information Sharing & Legal Radiocommunications

No Sale of Data: We will never sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties.

Served Public Safety Agencies: During official activations, operational data (call signs, staging status, ICS documentation) may be shared with Freestone County Emergency Management, the Freestone County Sheriff’s Office, Texas Division of Emergency Management (TDEM), and the National Weather Service.

Notice Regarding Amateur Radio Transmissions: Users are reminded that all transmissions made over amateur radio voice repeaters, simplex frequencies, APRS, or packet radio are open, unencrypted, public broadcasts by mandate of FCC Part 97 rules and cannot be kept confidential over radio airwaves.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v) Compliant)

Every registered member has the absolute right to view, correct, or request deletion of their account and personal profile data at any time.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion:

  1. In-App: Navigate to your Profile Screen and tap "Delete My Account".
  2. Via Direct Email: Send an email from your registered address to fcares@pm.me with the subject "Account Deletion Request", including your Name and Call Sign.

All personal profile credentials, phone numbers, and contact entries will be permanently deleted from active user databases within thirty (30) days of verified request.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is intended for certified amateur radio operators and emergency response personnel. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
